## Category: Database Internals
### **Write-Ahead Logging (WAL) and Durability**

WAL guarantees durability and atomicity by requiring all modifications to be logged sequentially in non-volatile storage before they are applied to the database pages. In the event of a crash, the log is replayed (Redo/Undo phases) to restore database consistency.
