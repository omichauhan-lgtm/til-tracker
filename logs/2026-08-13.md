### **Kafka Partitioning and Consumer Group Rebalancing**

Kafka achieves scale by partitioning topics. A consumer group distributes partitions among active consumer instances. If a consumer fails or joins, a rebalance occurs. Using static membership and cooperative sticky assignors reduces rebalance latency and minimizes message processing gaps.
