## Category: Storage Engines
### **SSTable Compaction: Size-Tiered vs Leveled**

Size-tiered compaction groups similarly sized SSTables and merges them into larger ones, risking high disk write-amplification. Leveled compaction divides disk into layers (L1, L2...), where each layer has a non-overlapping key space. It keeps read amplification low but requires constant merge operations.
